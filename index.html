Overlapping Action: The Secret to Natural Movement

Overlapping action makes animations feel alive. Hair, clothing, and limbs should not stop at the same time — they continue moving slightly after the main action stops. Use this principle to add realism to character walks, runs, and gestures.

Pro tip: Start by animating simple objects like flags or chains before applying it to characters.
